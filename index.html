lafspline

Least ambitious fit, P-spline version: a confidence region from a P-spline, then the simplest curve inside it.

Follows tautfit and the earlier project. P-splines after P. H. C. Eilers and B. D. Marx (1996), Statist. Sci. 11(2):89–121. Solver: HiGHS (MIT license), compiled to WebAssembly.
